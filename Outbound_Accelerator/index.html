How the SPIFF pays

Count outbound named-account SQAs in the quarter, subtract your segment threshold, multiply what is left by $150. Uncapped. Your core BDR plan is unchanged — this is extra upside on top.
Step 1 · count
Outbound SQAs
Outbound named-account SQAs delivered in the quarter — any opportunity segment. Population is Ent/MM BDR roles.
Step 2 · threshold
Ent 3 · MM 4
Same threshold for BDR and Senior BDR. Set below full quarterly quota so you can earn early.
Step 3 · pay
$150
Per SQA above the threshold. Uncapped.
Example
$300
Ent BDR with 5 outbound SQAs: (5 − 3) × $150 = $300.
